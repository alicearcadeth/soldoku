Give a familiar puzzle a shared finish

A reason to play again

Sudoku is usually a private score. Soldoku gives friends a shared grid, a recorded finish, and a leaderboard to compare. Our next product test is whether recurring rooms and shared results bring players back. Retention is not yet measured.

Product hypothesis, not a traction claim.

An entry players can inspect

The stake goes into escrow

Choose a preset or custom amount. Confirmed entrants solve the same grid when registration closes. The unique fastest valid finish claims the pool. Ties or no finishes refund entrants.

The server verifies gameplay and authorizes settlement; Solana holds the deposits.
Built and tested on Seeker

The wallet stays in Seed Vault

The Android shell loads Soldoku and hands wallet requests to Mobile Wallet Adapter. Connection, message authorization, transaction approval, background return, cancellation and recovery were exercised on a physical device.

The app does not hold the player's private key.
A completed financial loop

Deposits, payout and refunds

Physical winner test: two 1-token deposits; 2-token prize claimed; vault empty. Scheduled cron retained finalized settlement receipts. DAABEC94: refund returned 1 token; wallet 20 to 21; vault empty.

Funded tests and the 11 October refund are documented in the public repository.
