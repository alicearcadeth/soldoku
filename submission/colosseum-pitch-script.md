# Soldoku pitch script

English. Approximately 1 minute 45 seconds at a natural pace. Replace [Name] with your name. Record yourself speaking; this is separate from the product demo. Keep the finished video below two minutes.

## Spoken script

Hi, I'm [Name], and we're building Soldoku.

We start with a game people already understand: Sudoku. Soldoku turns that familiar puzzle into a mobile competition. Friends can enter the same room, solve the same grid, and compete for the fastest correct finish.

Players can enjoy Classic without a wallet, try a free competition, or enter a funded competition. In the funded flow, Solana holds the deposits in escrow. Our server verifies the moves and completion times, and the unique fastest finisher can claim the pool. Ties and no valid finishes lead to refunds.

We've built the full loop: create, deposit, solve, rank, settle, and claim. Our live demo uses real signed Devnet transactions, including three wallet deposits and a three-TEST winner payout. TEST has no cash value.

We've built the web experience, the Cloudflare backend, the Rust escrow program, and an Android shell. AI tools help us implement and debug, and we check the work through tests and live rehearsals. That gives us a working product we can put in front of players and improve from their feedback.

Today we're testing on Devnet. Mainnet is our next deployment target, after production token support, security checks, and mobile wallet verification.

We want winning to feel memorable, too: fireworks, matching sound, and a leaderboard players can share.

Our goal is simple: make Sudoku a competition people want to come back to. Try Soldoku at soldoku.com.

## Recording cues

- Look at the camera and speak at roughly 140–150 words per minute.
- Pause briefly after “We've built the full loop.”
- Say “three TEST” as the token name, rather than “three dollars.”
- Use your own name; no other personal details are needed in this script.
- You can record this in one take. The separate demo video already shows the product.
